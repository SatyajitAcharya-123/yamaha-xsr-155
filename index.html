01 — Design

Built on heritage. Designed for now.

Round lamp, teardrop tank, flat single-piece seat. The XSR 155 keeps the silhouette simple and lets the proportions do the talking.

  • HeadlampRound LED unit up front.
  • TankTeardrop, 10-litre, finished with Yamaha lettering.

  • TailCircular LED tail lamp.

02 — Engineering

Compact.Considered.

/ 01 Engine

155 cc. Liquid-cooled. VVA.

A single-cylinder, 4-valve SOHC engine with fuel injection and Variable Valve Actuation, matched to a six-speed gearbox with a wet multi-disc clutch. E20-fuel compatible.

18.4 PS@ 10,000 rpm
14.2 Nm@ 7,500 rpm
/ 02 Lighting

LED, front to back.

A round LED headlamp and a circular LED tail lamp keep the retro outline while using current lighting technology.

/ 03 Instrumentation

Round LCD, modern data.

Digital speedometer, tachometer and fuel gauge, with a fuel-consumption indicator. Y-Connect smartphone connectivity is listed on Yamaha India’s specification sheet.

/ 04 Chassis & suspension

Deltabox. Link-type rear.

A Deltabox frame with link-type rear suspension, 137 kg kerb weight and a 1,325 mm wheelbase.

1,325mm wheelbase
137kg kerb
/ 05 Wheels & tyres

17 inches. Disc front and rear.

Tubeless tyres on 17-inch wheels, with a 282 mm front and 220 mm rear disc and dual-channel ABS. Traction control is listed as standard.

/ 06 Ergonomics

810 mm. Upright. Light.

A flat single-piece seat at 810 mm and a 137 kg kerb weight. Ground clearance is listed at 120 mm — lower than its MT-15 sibling — so check the fit in person.

11 — FAQ

Good to know.

Is this the official Yamaha website?
No. This is an independent, informational site about the Yamaha XSR 155. For purchases, warranty, servicing, or official pricing, contact an authorised Yamaha dealer or Yamaha Motor India.
Where do the specifications come from?
From Yamaha India’s published specification sheet for the India-market model, cross-checked with press coverage. Other markets differ, and a few items vary between sources — those are flagged “verify”.
Does a test-ride request confirm an appointment?
No. A request only records your preferred place, date and time. It becomes an appointment only after you are contacted and a slot is agreed. This site does not guarantee availability.
What is the difference between “Email us” and the inquiry form?
“Email us” opens a draft in your own email app, and you must press send there. The inquiry form submits your message through this site and stores it for the owner to read.
Who can read my feedback?
Only the site owner, through a private dashboard. Feedback is not published. Reviews are different: they are public only after the owner approves them.
Why hasn’t my review appeared?
Every review is held for moderation first. Approved reviews appear in the Reviews section; others are not shown.
Are prices and colour availability listed here?
No. Prices and colour availability change and vary by dealer. Please confirm them directly with a dealer.

Privacy & legal

How your information is handled

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: contact details, messages, ratings, and (for test rides) phone number, location, date and time. Optional fields are optional.

Why

To read and respond to your message, arrange a test ride you requested, or moderate your review. Nothing is used for advertising.

Who sees it

The site owner only. Feedback, inquiries and test-ride requests are private. Reviews are public only after approval, showing your chosen display name — never your email.

Spam protection

A one-way hash of your network address is kept briefly to limit abuse. It is not used to identify you.

Your choices

Ask the owner (see Contact) to correct or delete your submission. The Email us button uses your own email app; this site does not see that message.
